# AI-005: Saved voice selection and startup greeting

Status: Implemented with separate request, transport and playback evidence.

## Delivered capability

Saved a validated next-call voice locally, required disconnect before changing an active voice and kept the greeting session-bound and at most once. No fabricated user utterance, automatic microphone or work assignment is used to trigger a greeting.

Catalog availability or speech-append acknowledgement alone did not prove audible output. A scoped native developer startup item supplied the greeting while preserving the underlying manager prompt. Human acoustic quality must be confirmed on the actual device.
